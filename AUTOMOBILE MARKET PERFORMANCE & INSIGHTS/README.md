🚗 Automobile Market Insights Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze the automobile market by providing insights into vehicle pricing, mileage, resale value, fuel type, insurance status, and brand performance. This dashboard enables users to explore market trends using dynamic filters and KPI visualizations, helping identify patterns and make data-driven decisions.

📌 Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of the automobile market by visualizing key performance metrics and trends. Users can filter the data by Manufacturing Year, State, Fuel Type, and Brand to gain detailed insights into vehicle performance and pricing.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze automobile market trends.
Compare average selling prices across different brands.
Identify top-priced bike models.
Analyze mileage based on fuel type.
Study resale prices with respect to insurance status.
Track pricing and registration trends over the years.
Provide an interactive dashboard for business insights.

📊 Insights
Hero and Bajaj have some of the highest average vehicle prices.
Electric vehicles demonstrate competitive mileage compared to conventional fuel types.
Vehicles with active insurance generally have higher resale values.
Average daily distance has increased for more recently registered vehicles.
Market prices show fluctuations over the years, indicating changing customer demand and market conditions.
